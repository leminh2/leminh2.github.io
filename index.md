# Welcome!

Hello! My name is **Minh Le**, and I am a **postdoctoral researcher** at the **Institute for Theoretical Sciences, Westlake University**, China.  
My research interests lie primarily in the field of **partial differential equations (PDEs)**, with a particular focus on **chemotaxis systems** and their applications in **biological modeling**.

I obtained my **Ph.D. in Mathematics** from **Michigan State University, USA (2025)**, where I worked under the supervision of **Professor Zhengfang Zhou**.  
My doctoral research centered on the **analysis of nonlinear PDEs** arising from biological and chemical processes, including problems related to **global existence**, **boundedness**, and **asymptotic behavior** of solutions.

Through my work, I aim to deepen the mathematical understanding of complex biological phenomena and contribute to the development of analytical techniques in **mathematical biology** and **fluid dynamics**.
